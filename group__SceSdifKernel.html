◆ SceSdifHostRegisters

struct SceSdifHostRegisters

Memory-mapped SD host-controller register block.

FW 3.60 maps a 0x1000-byte register memblock for each of the three controllers. The register prefix follows the SD Host Controller interface; the remainder of the mapping is unused by the reviewed SceSdif code.

◆ SceSdifContextPart

typedef struct SceSdifContextPart SceSdifContextPart

Opaque device-specific context.

FW 3.60 MMC, SD, and SDIO contexts all begin with a SceSdifContextPartBase, but use different layouts and storage sizes beyond that prefix: 0x398 bytes for MMC, 0xC0 bytes for SD, and 0xD38 bytes for SDIO. The context is owned by SceSdif and remains valid until that device is reset or reinitialized; callers must not free it.
